# Reservation Agent Guide

## Quick Reference

| Action | Key Steps |
|--------|-----------|
| New Booking | Verify availability → Collect guest info → Confirm details → Process payment → Send confirmation |
| Modification | Pull up reservation → Verify identity → Check availability → Update details → Confirm changes |
| Cancellation | Pull up reservation → Verify identity → Check policy → Process refund if applicable → Send confirmation |

---

## 1. Taking a New Reservation

### Required Information
- [ ] Full name (as it appears on ID)
- [ ] Contact phone number
- [ ] Email address
- [ ] Check-in date and time
- [ ] Check-out date / duration
- [ ] Number of guests
- [ ] Room/table/service type preference
- [ ] Special requests (accessibility, dietary, etc.)
- [ ] Payment method

### Booking Script
> "Thank you for calling [Company Name], this is [Your Name]. How may I assist you today?"

> "I'd be happy to help you with that reservation. May I have your preferred dates and the number of guests?"

> "I have [option] available for [dates]. The rate is [price] per night/person. Would you like me to book that for you?"

> "To complete your reservation, I'll need your full name, contact number, and email address."

> "Your confirmation number is [NUMBER]. You'll receive a confirmation email shortly. Is there anything else I can help you with?"

## 2. Modifying a Reservation

### Steps
1. Ask for confirmation number or name + date
2. Verify identity (last 4 digits of phone or email on file)
3. Confirm what changes are needed
4. Check availability for new dates/options
5. Explain any rate differences or fees
6. Process the modification
7. Provide new confirmation details

### Common Modifications
| Change Type | Policy |
|-------------|--------|
| Date change | Subject to availability; rate may change |
| Add guests | Check capacity limits; may incur additional charges |
| Room/service upgrade | Charge difference in rate |
| Name change | Allowed with valid reason; verify new guest info |

## 3. Cancellation Handling

### Cancellation Tiers
| Timeframe | Refund Policy |
|-----------|---------------|
| 72+ hours before | Full refund |
| 24-72 hours before | 50% refund |
| Less than 24 hours | No refund |
| No-show | Full charge + possible fee |

### Cancellation Script
> "I can help you with that cancellation. May I have your confirmation number?"

> "I've located your reservation for [date]. Based on our cancellation policy, [explain refund situation]. Would you like to proceed?"

> "Your reservation has been cancelled. Your refund of [amount] will be processed within 5-7 business days. Is there anything else I can help with?"

---

## 4. Handling Difficult Situations

### Overbooking
1. Apologize sincerely
2. Offer alternative dates or upgraded options
3. If no alternatives available, assist with competitor referral
4. Offer compensation (discount on future booking, complimentary service)

### Price Disputes
1. Pull up the original booking confirmation
2. Explain charges clearly, line by line
3. If error exists, correct immediately and apologize
4. If customer saw different price online, explain rate fluctuations
5. Offer to honor lower rate if within manager-approved threshold

### Complaints
1. Listen actively without interrupting
2. Acknowledge the concern: "I understand how frustrating that must be"
3. Apologize for the inconvenience
4. Offer a solution or escalate to supervisor
5. Follow up to ensure resolution

## 5. Special Requests

| Request Type | Action |
|--------------|--------|
| Accessibility needs | Note in reservation; confirm availability; coordinate with operations |
| Early check-in / Late check-out | Subject to availability; may incur fee |
| Celebrations (birthday, anniversary) | Note in reservation; coordinate surprises with team |
| Dietary restrictions | Document clearly; alert kitchen/service team |
| VIP / Loyalty members | Check status; apply appropriate benefits; prioritize requests |

---

## 6. Payment Processing

### Accepted Methods
- Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
- Debit cards
- Company/corporate accounts
- Gift cards/vouchers
- Third-party booking platforms (verify details)

### Security Reminders
- Never read full card numbers aloud
- Do not store card details in unsecured locations
- Verify billing address matches card
- Watch for fraud indicators (rushed transactions, unusual requests)

---

## 7. Escalation Guidelines

### When to Escalate to Supervisor
- Customer requests to speak with manager
- Refund amount exceeds your authorization limit
- Legal threats or mention of lawyers
- Safety or security concerns
- Complaints involving discrimination or harassment
- Situations requiring compensation beyond standard policy

### Escalation Process
1. Inform the customer: "Let me connect you with my supervisor who can better assist you"
2. Brief the supervisor on the situation before transferring
3. Document the interaction in the reservation notes
